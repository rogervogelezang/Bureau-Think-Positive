## Observation

## Emotion regulation

- Learning to recognise tension and the experience of emotions, gaining insight and processing
- Improving impulse control
- Learning skills or coping strategies for dealing with tension
- Recognising triggers
- Finding your own way to relax
- Acceptance and processing of emotions
- Constructive expression of emotions
