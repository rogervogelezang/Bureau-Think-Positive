## For working with stuck young people with a mild intellectual disability and a disharmonic development profile

A hands-on coach is a unique combination of support worker, coach and social pedagogue, and can be deployed in emergency situations. As a care professional, a hands-on coach first steps into the role of support worker, to experience on the floor where the heart of the situation lies. From that position, the coach helps the team build a clear picture of the situation and of the care recipient.

From this starting point, he then gets to work on the interventions that are needed. This is done using Design Thinking.
