Many young people struggle with a negative self-image. This causes all sorts of problems, because if you think you're worth less than someone else, you automatically think you're less capable too — less able to solve your problems, for instance, or less able to manage your emotions.

Strengthening a young person's self-image contributes to inner calm and to developing their strengths, which they can then use to make something of their life.

Discovering that you're capable of something creates a snowball effect. Through playful exercises and activities, we search out the young person's strengths. We do this step by step, so the young person has time to get used to their newfound strength.

Slowly, their perspective on their own abilities changes; slowly, the behavioural issues fade — and there you have it: a young person who knows what they can do, who believes in what they can do!
