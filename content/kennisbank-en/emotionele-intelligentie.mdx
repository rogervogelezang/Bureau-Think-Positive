In short, emotional intelligence is about how someone deals with their own emotions and those of others, how someone reacts (emotionally) to particular situations, and about their capacity for empathy. A high EQ — high emotional intelligence — is linked to success in society. A low EQ, on the other hand, can point to problems such as depression, alcohol abuse, eating disorders and aggressive behaviour.

Good cooperation between emotion and reason is essential when making decisions in life. Feeling counts at least as heavily as thinking when it comes to decision-making. At moments when someone is emotionally upset, they are not well placed to think clearly.

At the heart of emotional intelligence lies the interplay between feeling and thinking. When feeling and thinking are well attuned to one another, this appears to raise emotional intelligence.

## The five domains of emotional intelligence

- Knowledge of one's own emotions.
- Regulating and managing emotions.
- Self-motivation.
- Recognising the emotions of others.
- Dealing with others and social skills.
