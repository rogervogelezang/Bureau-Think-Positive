After years of working on units where care recipients who struggle considerably with keeping their emotions in check reside, a list was drawn up of the most common de-escalating interventions that colleagues use among themselves. This list was then divided into empathic de-escalating interventions and confronting and limit-setting interventions. All these techniques aim to prevent escalations and/or bring them to a good resolution.

The VUUR series therefore belongs among the empathic de-escalating interventions, alongside the PINTA and TRAAN series. These three groups of interventions form the basis of a crisis-response pyramid and have an accommodating, co-regulating character — they call for an empathic attitude from the care worker applying them.
