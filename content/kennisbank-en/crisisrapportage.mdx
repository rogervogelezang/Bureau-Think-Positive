Keep track of behavioural issues, aggression and hard-to-interpret behaviour in a separate crisis report. It's also useful for observing how a new care recipient is settling in.

## Structure of the report

The report starts with the basic details: the care recipient's name, day/date/shift, and the name of the person reporting. It then works through the following sections:

1. **Daily programme** — work, school, education, transport, rest moments.
2. **Lifestyle/network** — diet, smoking, exercise, sleep times, free time, pocket money, relationships.
3. **Interaction with fellow residents/support workers/others.**
4. **Psychological** — fill in an ABC for the behaviour in question.
5. **Support style.**
6. **Medical** — report on as-needed medication (what do you see, what do you hear, does it work, etc.). Fill in an ABC for this too.
7. **General course of events/notable points** that stood out.

## The ABC list

Also ask your colleague what they observed. For each moment, note down:

- **A: what preceded the behaviour**
- **B: behaviour**
- **C: what followed the behaviour**
- **Additional information**
