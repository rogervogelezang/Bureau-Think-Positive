## Learning to manage your emotions (emotion regulation)

Many young people struggle to regulate their emotions: some flare up quickly, others withdraw into their shell, and some never let on what they're feeling inside. Learning to manage your emotions is vital. It helps you build a balanced relationship with yourself and those around you, it helps you resolve difficult situations constructively, and it contributes to your overall wellbeing.
