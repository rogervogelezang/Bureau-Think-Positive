---
title: "The Law of Reciprocity"
description: "Cialdini's principle of reciprocity: why people automatically want to do something in return when they've received something or been granted a favour."
category: "Models & theories"
sourceSlug: "de-wet-van-wederkerigheid"
---

Cialdini says that we humans automatically want to do something in return when we've received something or been granted a favour. We want to repay our "debt". In other words: we treat others the way they've treated us. It's in our nature.
