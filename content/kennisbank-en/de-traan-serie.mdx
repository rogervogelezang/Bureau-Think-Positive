After years of working on units where care recipients live who have quite a bit of trouble keeping their emotions in check, we drew up a list of the most common de-escalating interventions that colleagues use among themselves. This list was then divided into empathic de-escalating interventions and confronting, limit-setting interventions. All these techniques are aimed at preventing escalations and/or bringing them to a positive resolution.

The TRAAN series therefore belongs to the empathic de-escalating interventions, alongside the PINTA and VUUR series. These three groups of interventions form the basis of a crisis-response pyramid and have a responsive, co-regulating character — they call for an empathic attitude from the care worker putting them into practice.
