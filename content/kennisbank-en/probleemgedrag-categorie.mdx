All the problem behaviour you (don't) want, in one overview.

## 1. Destructive behaviour

Destroying things, tearing (clothing), throwing or knocking things over, starting fires.

## 2. Physical problem behaviour

Regurgitating or spitting out food, eating inedible things, eating or drinking excessively, eating or drinking far too little, playing with or smearing faeces, deliberate incontinence, strongly self-stimulating behaviour, climbing on everything, no sense of danger, motor restlessness, hyperactivity, seeking out strong sensory stimuli.

## 3. Physical aggression

Spitting, pulling out hair, scratching, pinching, biting, banging, cutting, poking at ears or eyes, picking at skin or wounds, hitting, kicking, gripping tightly or choking, pushing over or knocking down.

## 4. Social problem behaviour

Stealing, drug addiction, running away, roaming, truancy.

## 5. Reactive problem behaviour

Protesting, resistance, evading authority, deliberately not listening or not responding, refusing to accept when something isn't allowed, unresponsive to correction, impulsive, erratic or disinhibited behaviour, testing boundaries or provoking, picking fights, teasing or bullying, not responding to other people's feelings, snatching or grabbing, frightening others, turning people against each other, demanding or claiming attention.

And much more.
