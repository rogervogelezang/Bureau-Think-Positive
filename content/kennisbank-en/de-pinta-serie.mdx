After years of working on units housing care recipients who find it quite difficult to keep their emotions in check, we drew up a list of the most common de-escalating interventions that colleagues use with one another. This list was then divided into empathic de-escalating interventions and confrontational, boundary-setting interventions. All these techniques are aimed at preventing escalations and/or bringing them to a good resolution.

The PINTA series therefore belongs to the empathic de-escalating interventions, alongside the VUUR and TRAAN series. These three groups of interventions form the basis of a crisis-response pyramid and have an accommodating, co-regulating character — they call for an empathic attitude from the care worker putting them into practice.
