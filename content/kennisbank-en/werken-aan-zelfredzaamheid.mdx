All self-reliance learning goals at a glance, organised into six domains:

1. Personal care
2. Household
3. Social care
4. Psychosocial care
5. Housing
6. Financial & administrative

## An example of possible goals

### I Personal care

- Keeping yourself clean
- Going to the GP or dentist on time
- Taking medication on time
- Using drugs and alcohol responsibly
- Protecting yourself against pregnancy and sexually transmitted infections
- Getting dressed and doing the laundry

And much more besides. An extensive list of possible learning goals per domain is included in the "Zelfredzaamheid Vaardigheden Algemeen" document.
