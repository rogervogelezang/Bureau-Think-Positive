In addition to our various training courses and coaching programmes, you can also come to Think Positive for guest lessons.

What better way to motivate students than to give them a glimpse of the profession in practice? It sparks ideas and helps them form an opinion about a particular occupation or sector. A guest lesson is a great way to get a first impression of a particular field and industry. This helps students find direction in their education.

Think Positive's guest lessons can cover topics such as:

- behavioural problems and aggression in general;
- how to deal with your own emotions when working with an aggressive client;
- which interventions you can use for problem behaviour and aggression;
- experiences and stories from the field;
- the right basic attitude when dealing with complex care needs or someone who has become stuck;
- methods for people with intellectual disabilities, such as ervaringsordening and Triple-C.
