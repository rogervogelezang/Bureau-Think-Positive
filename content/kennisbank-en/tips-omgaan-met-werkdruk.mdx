## 1. Always start your shift on time, arrive a little early if you can

That way you start from a place of calm. If you rush in, you bring time pressure with you from the outset.

## 2. When things get hectic: set priorities, together with your colleagues and clients

Don't try to just work harder and harder. That often costs you extra time, because it leads to mistakes.

## 3. Anticipate peak moments and work together

Busy spells and quiet spells both come with the job. You'll feel the strain less if you hand over properly and let go.

## 4. Take a moment to slow down, especially when you feel there's no time for anything

Count to ten, find a quiet spot for a moment, or sit down next to a client for five minutes. Slowing the pace down builds the calm you need to get everything back in order.

## 5. Let off steam, but don't overload your colleagues

Venting for a moment is fine. Complaining about time pressure costs time and raises the stress level.
