Aggression can shape everything around it — the atmosphere in a home, but also the development of the person struggling with their own aggression. Calling a halt to aggression in time, before it becomes a pattern, is a must. But sometimes we let it run on until it does become a pattern. Then the house, the living group or the ward we live and stay in becomes unsafe, and we have to do something about it. But what? Where do you start?

Leave that to the specialists. As crisis specialists, we deal with aggression issues every single day. We've seen it all, from aggression at home to aggression on high-security wards within care settings.

Our aggression coaches can help you manage aggression — your own and/or that of your family members — so it no longer takes centre stage in your life.

## Our approach

Through systematic observation and conversation, we first work out what the problem is and what's causing the aggression. We then talk with you about how to tackle it.

Tackling aggression is tailor-made work, and it calls for wisdom, courage and perseverance.

Sometimes advice is enough; often it isn't. Frequently we're required to step in ourselves to bring an unsafe situation to an end. At other times, training or a coaching programme is the best remedy. You only really know once you grasp how serious the situation is — and that calls for an analytical look at the situation, which is why we take the time at the start to talk through what's going on.

When it comes to aggression at home, sometimes there's no other way than to request assistance. This assistance can be provided by the police, Veilig Thuis, local psychiatric organisations or local crisis services.

In the past, we've achieved many results when it comes to stabilisation, aggression regulation and aggression management. You can count on us.
