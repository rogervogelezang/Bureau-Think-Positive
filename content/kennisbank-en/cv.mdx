## Coach/trainer at Bureau Think Positive — October 2011 to present (throughout the Netherlands)

Crisis intervention, coaching and advice.

Bureau Think Positive offers coaching programmes for professionals in (complex) care in the field of crisis intervention and clients whose cases have stalled. Private individuals can also turn to Bureau Think Positive for coaching and advice on psychological and behavioural issues.

## Interim (crisis) support worker/coach on a freelance basis at Astare, Kroek&Partners and Interim 24/7 — May 2013 to 2014

Secondment assignments on a project basis in care and support services throughout the Netherlands. From tbs institutions to JeugdzorgPlus.

## Interim (crisis) support worker at Koninklijke Kentalis (via Inzetbaar) — February 2012 to May 2013

Implementing the Total Communication method on a stalled unit for deaf and hard-of-hearing children with severe behavioural problems, intellectual disabilities and psychiatric disorders in an orthopaedagogical setting.

## Interim (crisis) support worker/coach at Inzetbaar — May 2010 to May 2013 (throughout the Netherlands)

Secondment assignments on a project basis in care and support services. Assignments range from straightforward housing support to crisis intervention and team coaching for dealing with clients whose cases the team has become stuck on. Sectors: intellectual disability care, psychiatry, youth care, special education, elderly care.

## Triple-C support worker at 's Heeren Loo VIC Workhome (via Inzetbaar) — June 2011 to January 2013

Helping set up an intensive treatment unit for supporting and treating people with intellectual disabilities who display hard-to-interpret behaviour (psychiatric) and sensory processing difficulties. Support provided according to the Triple-C method.

## Team coach and client support worker at Respect Zorggroep — June 2010 to October 2011 (Scheveningen)

Crisis intervention with stalled clients in elderly care, team coaching, support.

## Housing Support Worker at 's Heeren Loo — May 2008 to May 2010 (Westland)

Housing support and independent-living training for young people with a mild intellectual disability. Method: social competence model.

## AVO Teacher and Equip Trainer (aggression regulation) at the Rebound Project — September 2006 to May 2008 (Den Haag)

Training students whose progress had stalled, teaching skills to help them return to mainstream education. Building awareness of their own role in the bigger picture, aggression regulation, moral development, social skills.

## AVO Teacher, SOVA Trainer, Vocational Trainer at JWC — January 2002 to May 2005 (Den Haag)

A school for special education in a disadvantaged neighbourhood in Den Haag, an institution where more than 150 different nationalities were represented. Alongside passing on knowledge, teaching social and emotional skills and a good work attitude was an important aspect of the role. Tasks: mentoring, care studies teacher, General Education teacher, vocational trainer.
