Fancy a vitality boost? Then try this exercise, about self-image, how you see the other person, and wonder.

## Step 1

Write down 5 positive things you noticed or observed in your own attitude during your shift.

Then write down 3 positive things you noticed in your client(s).

Finish by writing down 1 thing that positively surprised you.

Do this at the end of your shift, two weeks in a row. Look back briefly at the end of each week. After two weeks, notice what effect keeping this log has had on how you see yourself and your clients.

## Step 2

After two weeks, do you notice any difference in your attitude and thinking? If so, what? Write this insight down on a sheet of A4 paper and put it up for a week somewhere you often pass, for example on the wall in your bedroom, in the loo, or near your computer.

## Step 3

Three weeks have now passed. Do you notice any difference in how you now approach your work, compared with three weeks ago?
