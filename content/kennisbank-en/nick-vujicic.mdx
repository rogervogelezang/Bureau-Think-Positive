Nick Vujicic was born without arms and legs, and nonetheless grew into a celebrated speaker on resilience and self-acceptance. In his talk on 'the gift of imperfection', he shows that your imperfections don't have to determine what you can achieve — a message that fits well with how we at Bureau Think Positive look at young people who have got stuck: not starting from the problem, but from what someone does have to offer.

That's why we're glad to share his story as a source of inspiration in our training sessions and talks about resilience.
