## My drive

"My sister was born with the umbilical cord wrapped tightly around her neck, and this event shaped a large part of her life." Growing up alongside her taught him a great deal.

## Background

After completing his studies and a two-and-a-half-year trip around the world, he settled in deprived neighbourhoods of The Hague. There he worked for seven years as a teacher and trainer in special education and on projects for pupils with serious behavioural issues.

"The lessons from the street grounded me and shaped my direction." He then followed the common thread of behavioural issues across the whole country: from mental health care (GGZ) units to crisis units at forensic psychiatric (tbs) clinics.

He increasingly received challenging assignments involving people who had become stuck, and the introduction of new methods. After years working for specialist secondment agencies, he started his own business in 2011.

## About me

"You don't just become a crisis specialist/emotion coach." Although he completed a higher professional education (hbo) qualification in social pedagogy (agogiek), he argues that no training exists for dealing with escalations and crisis situations.

He became a crisis specialist through experience and personal development. "Everything I've experienced has shaped me, has made me an expert." Nowadays he also works outside care as an emotion coach and crisis coach.
