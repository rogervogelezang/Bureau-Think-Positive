## Introduction

- Ask participants what they expect to learn.
- Say a little about myself and about Think Positive.
- Say something about the training.
- Supporting belongs in that list: supporting, confronting, setting limits — together we call this 'de-escalating'.
- Briefly explain the pyramid of escalating behavioural problems.
- Question: what is de-escalating?
- Supporting, the basic attitude.
- Confronting, the basic attitude.
- Setting limits, the basic attitude.

## Emotions

- Question: which basic emotions do we know?
- Game exercise: recognising basic emotions.
- Positive (healthy) and negative (unhealthy) emotions.
- Question: what is problem behaviour?
- Explain the link between 'negative emotion' and 'problem behaviour'.
- Game exercise: naming negative emotions.

## Supporting, the interventions

- What is a supportive intervention?
- Which fourteen supportive interventions do we know?
- The supportive interventions in brief.
- Practise the empathic de-escalating interventions from the PINTA, VUUR and TRAAN series.

## Closing

- Are there any questions?
- What will you take away from this training?
- Feedback round.
