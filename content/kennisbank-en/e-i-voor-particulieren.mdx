## Managing emotions wisely is for individuals too

A bit of emotional intelligence can benefit anyone, think of:

- the hothead who wants to keep his temper under control
- the grown woman who wants to learn to control her buying impulses
- the father who wants to learn to treat his family with more warmth
- the student who wants to boost his motivation to study
- the teenager who wants to learn to be more considerate of his parents

## What does managing emotions wisely mean?

A high EQ appears to boost your chances of success. For a long time, IQ (intelligence, cognitive ability) was thought to be an important predictor of success in society. But over the years it has become clear that people who can handle their own emotions and those of others well have a better chance of succeeding in life.

## Want to learn to manage your emotions wisely?

Boost your emotional intelligence and learn to:

- control your impulses so you can be more productive
- cope better with frustration
- be more socially skilled and more reliable
- become more effective and assertive
- approach life with more optimism
- achieve long-term goals
- stay more motivated in life
- read others (customers, colleagues) better
- collaborate better
- negotiate better
