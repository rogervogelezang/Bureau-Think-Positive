Dear parents, carers and children,

Providing quality matters to us. To find out what our parents, carers and children think, we have decided to carry out a quality survey.

Through this survey, we hope to gain a better understanding of what is going on day to day and where our strengths and areas for improvement lie.

In the questionnaire, you can share your opinion on various aspects of our agency. It contains around 30 child-focused questions covering everything from the working climate at de Ruimte/Kievit to general social safety; completing it takes no more than 15 to 20 minutes. There are also 60 questions aimed at parents and carers.

By 'de Ruimte' we mean the location where the child receives the most support — this could also be school, or home.

Questions are answered on a scale of -- / - / + / ++ / ?, meaning: disagree / more agree than disagree / more disagree than agree / agree. You can mark the appropriate box with a cross.

Completing this survey gives us the opportunity to gauge the working climate within our organisation. We would appreciate it if you could fill in these questionnaires and return them to us at info@bureauthinkpositive.com.
