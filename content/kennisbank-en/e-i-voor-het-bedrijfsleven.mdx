## Managing emotions wisely is for business too

Everyone can use a bit more emotional intelligence. Think of:

- the department manager at a department store who wants to encourage customer-friendliness among staff
- the manager who wants to make their team more productive
- the salesperson who wants to close a deal
- the aggression trainer who wants to teach their team to de-escalate
- the life coach who wants to attract more clients

## What does managing emotions wisely mean?

A high EQ appears to increase the chances of success. For a long time, IQ (intelligence, cognitive ability) was thought to be an important predictor of success in society. But over the years, it has become clear that people who can manage their own emotions and those of others well have a greater chance of succeeding in life.

## Want to learn to manage your emotions wisely?

Boost your emotional intelligence and learn to:

- control your impulses to be more productive
- cope better with frustration
- be more socially skilled and reliable
- become more effective and assertive
- approach life with more optimism
- achieve long-term goals
- stay more motivated in life
- read others, clients and colleagues, better
- collaborate better
- negotiate better
