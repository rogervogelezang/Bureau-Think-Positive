Bureau Think Positive provides specialised in-home support and makes a collection of tools available to care professionals. These tools are organised into categories.

## Available tools

- **Emotional intelligence** — the ability to recognise feelings and deal with them.
- **Problem behaviour categories** — behavioural expressions classified by type.
- **Quality of life questionnaire** — analysis of care recipients' quality of life.
- **Resilience tips** — thirty strategies for dealing with emotions.
- **Sandwich technique** — a gentle way to confront the care recipient with undesirable behaviour.
- **Self-reliance skills** — learning goals across six domains.
- **Leary's Rose** — breaking free from fixed patterns of interaction.
- **The Traffic Light** — a signalling plan for children.
- **Pictograms** — visual support.
- **Signalling plans** — de-escalating ways of working.
- **Behavioural analysis** — a structured approach to complex behavioural issues.
