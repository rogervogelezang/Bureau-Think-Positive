Sometimes de-escalating support isn't enough, and the care recipient's agitation and unwanted behaviour build up to the point where, as a carer or support worker, you have no option but to work towards behaviour change.

But how do you make sure the care recipient actually listens to you? How do you approach them? What do you need to pay attention to when addressing them? Which techniques do you use? And how do you respond to (verbal) aggressive behaviour?

During this training, spread across two half-days, you're given interventions for tackling inappropriate and unacceptable behaviour.

Interventions from everyday practice — some will look familiar, think of:

- The stop instruction
- Feedback on negative behaviour
- Addressing personal responsibility
- Problematising
- Offering behavioural suggestions

And plenty more confrontational and limit-setting techniques to bring about behaviour change in your care recipient in an assertive way.

Alongside practising these interventions, we also take a moment to reflect on our own attitude, because being aware of your own attitude is a must when working with aggression.
