Alongside our various workshops, you can also come to Think Positive for talks and presentations. Talks are an excellent way to explore a theme and inspire your staff.

These can cover:

- building resilience
- working from compassion
- working from a positive mindset
- the power of gratitude
- the power of vulnerability
- emotional regulation
- distance and closeness
- making genuine connection
- grounding and centring

Of course, you are also always welcome to suggest your own theme.
