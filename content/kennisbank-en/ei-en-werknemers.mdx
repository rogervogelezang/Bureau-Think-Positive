title: "Emotional intelligence and employees"
description: "Why emotional intelligence, team building and adaptability are becoming increasingly important for employees within shrinking organisations."

As organisations shrink due to waves of cutbacks and the reorganisations that come with them, the employees who remain take on more responsibility and become more visible. Where a mediocre employee could once easily hide a short temper or shyness, qualities such as managing your own emotions, functioning well during a meeting, teamwork and self-awareness now count and show more than ever.

As the business world changes, so do the qualities needed to excel. Comparing the data on the skills top performers needed to have over the past decades, it turns out that two abilities which contributed little to success in the seventies and eighties became crucial in the nineties and in the new millennium: team building and adapting to change.

Employees and employers are beginning to realise that success today requires more than average or high intelligence or professional expertise, and that we need a different kind of skill to survive — and certainly to get ahead in the increasingly turbulent job market of the future. Inner, human qualities such as resilience and decisiveness, optimism and adaptability are taking on new meaning.
