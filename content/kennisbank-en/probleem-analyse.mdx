title: "Problem analysis for complex behaviour"
description: "Why a thorough problem analysis is the basis for a good approach to difficult, complex problem behaviour, rather than quick assumptions."
category: "Tools & exercises"

---

When you're faced with a difficult, complex form of problem behaviour, it's wise to approach it thoroughly. Don't jump straight in with assumptions and start working from them, because you can easily end up down a dead-end track.

It's better to make a thorough analysis of the starting situation:

- What is the history behind it?
- What external factors could be influencing the situation?
- Has the problem occurred before, and how was it dealt with then?

Follow the matrix and you'll find your way to the solution.
