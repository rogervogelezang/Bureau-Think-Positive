---
title: "The Traffic Light"
description: "A child-level signalling plan that helps children recognise their own emotions and know what's expected of them when feelings run high."
category: "Tools & exercises"
sourceSlug: "het-stoplicht"
---

The traffic light helps children learn to recognise their own emotions. It also gives them practical tools for how to act when emotions run high. This is really useful, because it means they know what's expected of them — which saves a lot of unrest and confusion.
