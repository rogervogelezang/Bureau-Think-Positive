## 1-on-1 sport & outdoor activities

Sport is ideally suited to teaching all kinds of skills. We use sport with young people to help them learn to deal with frustration and to develop their social skills.

How does a young person respond to setbacks? Do they give up? Do they put themselves down, or do they use the situation to build their resilience?

As part of our in-home support, we offer sport and outdoor activities.

Together with parents and the referring agency, we draw up an action plan. This briefly describes what we'll be working on over the coming months and which goals we'll be working towards. This differs for every young person.

After that, we work with the young person to find a (sporting) challenge that suits them. Through this challenge, we help them reach a higher level — step by step, bit by bit, each at their own pace.

It's wonderful to see how quickly these children's confidence grows the moment they discover their own strength!

Besides sport, taking on outdoor activities is also a way to push your boundaries and get to know yourself. Setting off on an adventure together on the beach or in the woods — there's so much to experience, and a whole world opens up for them.
