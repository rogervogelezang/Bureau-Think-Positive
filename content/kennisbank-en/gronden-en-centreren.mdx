In grounding and centring, you go in search of the centre, or centre of gravity, of the body, in order to hold onto it. The centre of gravity is located roughly five centimetres below the navel.

When someone is centred there, they are said to be in balance both psychologically and physically. He (or she) then has greater focus and concentration.

By practising with tools such as an indo board and other 'rock-and-water' training aids, we search for and find that centre. By directing our breathing there, we learn to build calm into our body, so we can stand firm on our own two feet.

This training consists of three one-hour lessons, spread over three weeks, and is suitable for the care recipient who wants to experience more calm by staying grounded in themselves. Ideal for anyone who wants to learn how to deal with ignoring other people's negative behaviour.
