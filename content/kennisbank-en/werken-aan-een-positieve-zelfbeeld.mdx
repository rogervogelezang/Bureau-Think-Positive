Working on a positive self-image happens in two phases: first we map out the situation, then we get to work specifically on the self-image itself.

## Observation

- Getting acquainted
- Observation
- Specifying the problem situation and problem behaviour
- Analysis of strengths and areas for improvement
- Determining a strategy for the problem situation and problem behaviour

## Self-image

- Strengthening self-confidence
- Improving self-image/body image
- Gaining insight into one's own abilities and qualities
- Developing a realistic sense of self and identity
- Shaping one's life story
