## Disharmonic development profile and getting stuck

More and more, we are seeing a particular 'target group' come into view: the young man (or woman) with a mild intellectual disability and a disharmonic development profile. Unfortunately, he or she usually only comes into view once it's already too late.

A disharmonic intelligence profile means that the IQ test shows a considerable gap between verbal IQ and performance IQ. In people with a mild intellectual disability and a disharmonic intelligence profile, the verbal IQ is always higher than the performance IQ. As a result, this target group is often overestimated in what they can actually do.

## What does it mean when verbal IQ is higher than performance IQ?

For these young people, processing verbal information (by listening) clearly comes more easily than processing performance-based information (by seeing).

They struggle with the skills needed to solve problems on the spot — in other words, with getting an overview of a task — but can apply things they have learned through experience relatively easily.

They find it hard to copy actions and to carry out spoken or written instructions. They can often talk you through something, but not actually do it.

Because these young people come across as highly verbal — read: 'smart' — they are often overestimated when it comes to actually doing things.
