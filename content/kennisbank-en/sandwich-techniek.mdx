---
title: "The sandwich technique"
description: "A conversation technique for confronting a care recipient about their behaviour by embedding the confrontation between two layers of understanding."
category: "Tools & exercises"
sourceSlug: "sandwich-techniek"
---

If you want to confront the care recipient about their behaviour, you can use a technique in which you show understanding for the reasons the care recipient has given for that behaviour.

The technique begins by showing understanding, after which you confront the care recipient, and then (if needed) show understanding once more.

The confrontation is embedded here between two layers of understanding — hence the name: the sandwich technique.
