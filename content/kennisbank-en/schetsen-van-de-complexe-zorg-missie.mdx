Everyone is allowed to be here, whatever difficulties you bring with you, whether you cooperate with your care or not, whether you're trustworthy or aggressive — you are allowed to be here. I will be there for you, I won't turn you away, I accept you for who you are, with all your imperfections, with the unpleasant smells, your little tricks, your inappropriate way of communicating, your unreasonableness. But also with your laughter and all the good that's in you: your wishes, your longings, your joy. I laugh along with you.

I accept you in your entirety, because you are allowed to be here just as you are. You are beautiful just as you are.

And from here, from this connection, born from an attitude of total acceptance, we look towards the future — towards your future, on the way to better care. I listen to you, actively, and I take the time. I try to put myself in your situation, however difficult that may be. I learn to speak your language, however difficult that may be, searching for the message behind the message. From an appropriate distance — or rather, closeness — I support you and offer you alternatives and, if you wish, suggestions for behaviour too. So that you can reach your goal with satisfaction, without unnecessarily walking over other people.

I support you so that you feel safe — safe enough to form connections and express your true wishes. Together, hand in hand, we work towards goals, your goals. I show you what's possible, what's achievable, and step by step I learn to let you go, because you can stand on your own two feet. I watch your steps quicken, and off you go: autonomous, free. I say goodbye, I wave to you, and wish you happiness.

*Your support worker and coach*
