All training courses, courses and workshops are delivered in-company. This means the training takes place at your location — in your care organisation, hospital, residential group or on the ward.

The advantage of this is that your participants have no travel time, and the training is held on a date and time of your choosing.

Naturally, you arrange a suitable training venue yourself with enough space to practise, as well as coffee, tea and lunches where needed. You also provide a flip chart, a projection screen and a projector for the instruction.

For training care recipients within an organisation (for example the grounding and centring training), a space is needed where physical exertion is possible, such as a small gym or dojo.

To safeguard effectiveness, Think Positive works with small groups of a maximum of eight participants per training.

Rates vary per training — ask for a price quote or quotation.
